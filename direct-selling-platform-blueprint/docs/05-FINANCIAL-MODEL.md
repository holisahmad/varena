# 05 — Financial Model and Unit Economics

All values are IDR. Illustrative only; exclude tax, payment gateway, packaging variations, shipping subsidy, returns, marketing, salaries, and other overhead unless stated.

## 1. First pack
- Retail price: 500,000
- HPP: 300,000
- Direct sponsor: 50,000
- Development reserve: up to 100,000 (10 x 10,000)
- Residual after full reserve: 50,000

| Item | IDR |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | 500,000 |
| HPP | (300,000) |
| Direct sponsor | (50,000) |
| Development reserve | (100,000) |
| Residual before overhead | 50,000 |

At only 8 available points: 80,000 distributed, 20,000 unallocated. Keep the 20,000 separately classified until accounting/legal policy approves recognition.

## 2. Repeat sachet
| Item | IDR |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | 45,000 |
| HPP | (30,000) |
| Gross margin before commission | 15,000 |
| 10 x Rp2,000 bonus | (20,000) |
| Residual | (5,000) |

Rp2,000 per level is loss-making under current HPP.

Simulation only:
| Bonus / point | Total for 10 | Residual before overhead |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | 5,000 | 10,000 |
| 750 | 7,500 | 7,500 |
| 1,000 | 10,000 | 5,000 |
| 1,200 | 12,000 | 3,000 |
| 1,500 | 15,000 | 0 |
| 2,000 | 20,000 | -5,000 |

Recommended model: compute a safe maximum bonus from actual contribution margin:
`max_total_commission = selling_price - HPP - payment_cost - tax_reserve - fulfilment_cost - returns_reserve - operating_reserve - target_contribution`
Then divide among eligible levels according to approved rule. Do not assume equal distribution if plan says otherwise.

## 3. Pack/sachet consistency
A pack price of 500,000 implies Rp50,000 per sachet inside the pack, while repeat price is Rp45,000. Document whether:
- sachet-only repeat sale is a distinct SKU/price,
- pack discount is intentional,
- the same HPP applies to individual sachets,
- packaging and fulfillment costs differ.

## 4. Ledger accounting concept
Use double-entry postings. Example commission accrual:
- Debit commission expense / marketing expense.
- Credit member commission payable.
Payout:
- Debit member commission payable.
- Credit bank/cash clearing.
Unallocated reserve:
- Keep a dedicated liability/suspense account until lawful/accounting recognition is approved.
Exact chart of accounts must be reviewed by company accountant.

## 5. Required reports
- Net sales, gross sales, discount, tax, shipping.
- HPP/COGS.
- Commission accrued by reward type.
- Commission paid/held/unallocated/reversed.
- Contribution margin by SKU and channel.
- Refunds and chargebacks.
- Payout payable aging.
- Operational allocations with authorization trail.
