# 01 — Product Overview

## 1. Product vision
Platform penjualan produk dan pengelolaan jaringan referral untuk perusahaan, koperasi, tim operasional, admin, leader, mitra, serta konsumen umum. Platform mengelola katalog, transaksi, jaringan sponsor/placement, komisi, saldo, pencairan, dan audit.

## 2. Product goals
- Membuat pembelian produk mudah dari mobile maupun desktop.
- Mengatribusikan transaksi ke referral yang sah.
- Menyediakan pengelolaan sponsor tree dan placement tree yang terpisah.
- Menghitung komisi sesuai marketing plan berversi.
- Menjamin saldo dan komisi dapat direkonsiliasi.
- Memberi visibilitas operasional tanpa membuka data di luar kewenangan.
- Mengurangi pekerjaan manual dan kesalahan perhitungan.

## 3. User groups
1. Owner: pengawasan bisnis, laporan, approval tingkat tertinggi.
2. Koperasi: akses sesuai mandat dan ruang lingkup.
3. Operasional: pengelolaan operasional dan akun operasional.
4. Admin 1/Admin 2: administrasi, verifikasi, layanan mitra/pesanan.
5. Leader 1/2/3: pengelolaan jaringan sesuai scope.
6. Mitra: referral, jaringan yang boleh dilihat, pesanan, komisi, wallet.
7. Konsumen: pembelian tanpa kewajiban menjadi mitra.
8. Finance reviewer: verifikasi dan approval pembayaran/pencairan.
9. Auditor: akses baca terbatas untuk pemeriksaan.

Role organisasi level 1–8 tidak otomatis berarti semua role memperoleh bonus. Hak ekonomi ditentukan oleh aturan komisi terpisah.

## 4. MVP scope
- Public website dan product catalog.
- Referral landing/attribution.
- Consumer checkout.
- Member registration and member portal.
- Sponsor relationship dan placement relationship.
- Placement management dengan approval/audit.
- Product/order/payment status.
- Commission calculation engine.
- Wallet ledger.
- Withdrawal request dan admin approval.
- Basic reports dan audit logs.

## 5. Out of scope MVP
- Native Android/iOS application.
- Multi-country/multi-currency.
- Multi-company SaaS tenancy kecuali dibutuhkan organisasi.
- AI-based commission decisions.
- Automated bank disbursement tanpa approval.
- Complex warehouse/ERP integration.
- Binary/matrix compensation plans.
- Automatic reassignment of historical network.
- Public exposure of full downline personal data.

## 6. Product success metrics
- Checkout completion rate.
- Paid orders and repeat purchase rate.
- Referral attribution accuracy.
- Commission calculation accuracy (100% test fixtures).
- Duplicate commission incidents (target zero).
- Payout reconciliation variance (target zero).
- Order cancellation/refund rate.
- Page performance and API reliability.
- Support resolution time.

## 7. Product principles
- Product-first, not recruitment-first.
- Explain prices, terms, and rewards transparently.
- No forced personal purchase as a payout unlock unless legal review explicitly approves a lawful, consumer-protective policy; current baseline is no personal-purchase requirement.
- No misleading income claims.
- Privacy by default.
- Human approval for money leaving company control.
